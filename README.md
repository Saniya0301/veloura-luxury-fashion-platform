### **About The Atelier**

**VELOURA** modernizes classic silhouettes with contemporary feminine confidence. Designed at the convergence of architectural symmetry and natural fluid motion, this platform showcases limited curations collaborated directly with certified eco-conscious mills in North Italy and hand-finishing guilds across North India.

---

### **Key Features**

* **Interactive 3D Emblem**: Features an interactive 3D Atelier signature emblem with smooth tilt & spin cursor interactions.
* **Integrated Style AI**: Dedicated AI integration assistant for personalized wardrobe styling and curation advice.
* **Editorial Lookbook**: Interactive multi-spread visual diary featuring modern silhouettes and quiet luxury outfits.
* **Fluid Experience**: Minimalist UI built for seamless navigational elegance, optimized dynamic drapery showcasing, and micro-interactions.
